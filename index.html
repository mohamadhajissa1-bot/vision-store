05 Support

Questions,
answered.

Can't find what you need? Open a ticket on our Discord and the team will help you out.

Read the docs Open a ticket

Sign in to keymaster.fivem.net with the same Cfx.re account you used at checkout and open Granted Assets. Your purchase is listed there, ready to download. Step-by-step guide: Buying and downloading.

Each script lists its requirements and supported frameworks in its description. Open a script to check before you buy.

Scripts are delivered through the Cfx.re asset escrow system. Config files and other editable parts stay open so you can customise them.

FiveM assets are granted to a Cfx.re account. Signing in tells Tebex which account should receive your purchase, so it can be delivered to Keymaster automatically.

Start with the installation guides in our documentation. Still stuck? Join our Discord and open a support ticket with your Cfx.re username and the script name. We'll help you get it running.

Tebex is our official reseller and handles payment, billing support and refunds under its own terms.
